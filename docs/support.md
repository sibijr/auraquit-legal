## Common questions

### How do I cancel my subscription?
iOS **Settings → your name → Subscriptions → AuraQuit**. Apple owns billing, so
cancelling from inside the app is not possible.

### How do I delete my data?
In AuraQuit: **Me → Erase all my data**. This clears your history on the device
and in your account, and you stay signed in.

To remove your account entirely: **Me → Delete account**. Deleting your account
does **not** cancel billing — cancel in iOS Settings as above.

### I already paid, but it asks me to subscribe
Your subscription is tied to the Apple ID that paid, and to the AuraQuit account
you were signed into. Sign in with the same provider you used originally (Apple
or Google), then tap **Restore Purchases** on the subscription screen.

### Motion & Fitness isn't working
The Walk activity counts steps. If you declined the permission, enable it in
**iOS Settings → AuraQuit → Motion & Fitness**. A manual counter is always
available, so the activity works either way.

### Is AuraQuit medical advice?
No. AuraQuit is a wellbeing tool. It does not diagnose or treat, and it is not a
substitute for advice from a clinician.
